# Clarify consequential knowledge decisions

Use this procedure when competing domain meanings, ownership choices or migration behavior materially change the next action. Reuse settled decisions and resolve ordinary implementation choices within authorized scope.

Read [domain modeling](domain-modeling.md). Determine which unresolved questions depend on others. Investigate factual prerequisites through [knowledge research](knowledge-research.md); ask the maintainer for choices that source and evidence cannot settle.

Present a small set of currently answerable choices, with a recommendation and concrete trade-offs. Resolve prerequisite questions before asking dependent ones. Record the chosen meaning in the existing glossary or decision owner once it is settled. Silence or a preselected option is not a decision.

Finish when the requested choice is explicit and its downstream implications are recorded. Continue already authorized independent work while a required answer is pending. Clarification does not introduce a universal approval phase or require reapproval of settled scope.

Adapted from [grill-with-docs](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/grill-with-docs) and its [grilling dependency](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grilling), checked 2026-10-04. z-shell uses concise consequential questions rather than exhaustive interviews for every edit.
